1. Who is responsible for your data

1.The controller of the personal data processed in connection with the operation of the VivaPilot App (the “App”) is Damian Gałka, a natural person conducting unregistered business activity; correspondence address: ul. Przybyszewskiego 209B/9, 93-120 Łódź, Poland, e-mail address: dg.komunikacja@gmail.com (the “Publisher”).
2.The Publisher processes data that it receives itself: from correspondence, from the Store in connection with the purchase of the Full Version, and from reviews published in the Store (section 3). The Publisher does not operate servers to which the App connects and has no access to data saved in the App on your device (section 4).
3.The parties to which the App connects (the provider of your Content Source, the Store, Google in connection with ads and Android system services) process data as separate controllers, under their own rules (sections 5 and 6).
4.For personal data matters, you can write to the e-mail address or the correspondence address given in paragraph 1.
5.Terms written with a capital letter (for example Content Source, Full Version, Store, Trial Period) have the meaning given to them in the App’s Terms of Use.

2. Key information

  • The App does not require you to create an account with the Publisher.
  • Your Content Sources (including logins and passwords), settings, favourites, lists and viewing history are saved in the App on your device. The Publisher has no access to them (section 4).
  • The Publisher receives your data when you write to the Publisher and, to a limited extent, also from the Store when you purchase the Full Version (section 3).
  • The App connects to the server of your Content Source and, when you use certain functions, also to the services of other parties (section 5).
  • If the App displays ads, the data for ads is processed by Google as a separate controller. You decide on the storing of information on your device and on personalised ads in the consent form (section 6).
  • The Publisher has not added analytics or tracking tools to the App (section 7).

3. Data processed by the Publisher

Correspondence

Data: e-mail address, first name and surname (if you provide them), the content of messages and any attachments you include.

Purpose and legal basis: replying to your message and helping you use the App – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the matter concerns a contract; in other cases Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (the Publisher’s legitimate interest: replying to messages).

How long: up to 12 months after the matter is closed. If the message concerns a complaint, withdrawal from a contract or claims, the periods given below apply.

Complaints, withdrawal from the contract and refunds

Data: data from correspondence, a description of the matter, purchase data (for example the order number) and, if the refund is made by bank transfer, the bank account number.

Purpose and legal basis: handling the complaint, handling the withdrawal from the contract and refunding the payment – Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (obligations under the Act of 30 May 2014 on Consumer Rights) and Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

How long: until the limitation period for claims under the contract expires.

Purchase data passed on by the Store

Data: order number, date, product, price, currency and country and, in some cases, the buyer’s e-mail address or name, to the extent that the Store makes them available.

Where it comes from: from the Store (Google Play), when you purchase the Full Version.

Purpose and legal basis: confirming the purchase and handling refunds and complaints – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; keeping records and tax settlements – Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

How long: data needed for tax settlements – 5 years from the end of the year in which the tax payment deadline expired; data needed to handle complaints – until the limitation period for claims under the contract expires.

Reviews in the Store

Data: your user name in the Store and the content of the review you publish about the App, if the Store allows a review to be posted, and the Publisher’s reply.

Where it comes from: from the Store.

Purpose and legal basis: reading the review and replying to it – Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (the Publisher’s legitimate interest: contact with Users and development of the App).

How long: for as long as the review is available in the Store.

Establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims

Data: data from the categories above, needed in the given matter.

Purpose and legal basis: protecting the Publisher’s rights – Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

How long: until the limitation period for the claims expires.

Additional information

1.Providing data is voluntary. Without an e-mail address or a correspondence address, the Publisher cannot reply to a message, and without purchase data the Publisher may be unable to find the transaction in the case of a complaint or a withdrawal from the contract.
2.The Store and Google AdMob make aggregate statistics about the App available to the Publisher, for example the number of installations, countries, device models and system versions, crash reports (if sending of diagnostic data to Google is switched on in the device settings), and the number of ad impressions and ad revenue. They do not contain data that allows the Publisher to identify a particular User.
3.The Publisher does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
4.The recipients of data may be: the provider of the e-mail service used by the Publisher (Google, the Gmail service), the Store (when handling a refund), the Publisher’s bank (in the case of a refund by transfer), parties providing the Publisher with legal or accounting help, if this is needed in the given matter, and public authorities, if required by law.

4. Data saved on your device

The App saves on your device’s storage the data needed for it to work. It does not send it to the Publisher, and the Publisher has no access to it. This is:

  • the addresses, user names and passwords of your Content Sources (Xtream Codes, M3U, the EPG programme guide address);
  • the catalogue of channels, films and series downloaded from your Content Sources, and thumbnails and posters (cache, up to 250 MB);
  • favourites, own lists, history and viewing progress;
  • settings, for example language, screen saver, hidden categories, preferred audio and subtitle tracks, buffer mode, and the selected genres, production periods and mode of the “Suggestions to watch” function;
  • folders with video files that you point to (the “Folder” function);
  • a technical log of the App’s last crash (a description of the error and the App’s last technical operations, for example the identifier of the content being played); logins, passwords and tokens contained in addresses are replaced in it with masking characters, and the record is not sent anywhere and the App has no function for sharing it;
  • information on the course of the Trial Period and on the purchase of the Full Version, and the record of acceptance of the Terms of Use: the date and time, the version numbers of the Terms of Use and the Policy, the declaration of legal age and, after a purchase, also the transaction number from the Store. You can view it and save it to a file in the App’s Settings.
1.The “Suggestions to watch” function selects items on your device, from the catalogue of your Content Source and your favourites. The App does not pass this data outside the device and does not create a profile on its basis. Only your selection of genres, periods and mode is saved.
2.If you have automatic Google backup switched on, the date of the first launch of the App (the start of the Trial Period) is saved in it. The same date may also be transferred when Google transfers data to a new phone. The backup is kept by Google, under its own rules. The other data in this section, including the passwords to Content Sources, is not covered by the Google backup.
3.Passwords to Content Sources are particularly sensitive. They can be seen by persons who use your device, so protect access to it (section 11).
4.You can delete the data in this section by clearing the App’s data in the system settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Connections to the services of other parties

The App connects to the servers of other parties only in the situations described in this section. These parties process data as separate controllers, under their own rules. The connections do not pass through the Publisher’s servers; the Publisher does not operate such servers.

Your Content Source

The App connects directly to the servers whose addresses you enter into the App (Xtream Codes, M3U list, EPG guide). The server receives technical connection data (for example your device’s IP address) and your login details and processes them under the rules of its provider. The server may be located outside the European Economic Area.

Background refresh. About once every 12 hours, when the device has an internet connection, the App may download the current catalogue from your Content Sources, even when you are not using the App. The Source’s server then receives the device’s IP address and login details, and the connection uses data transfer. You can switch off background refresh in the App’s Settings.

Images. The App downloads logos, thumbnails and posters from the addresses given in your Content Source. They may lead to the servers of other parties, which then receive the IP address of your device.

Voice search

After you press the microphone button, the App launches the speech recognition service selected in your device’s system (for example a Google service). The voice recording is processed by the provider of that service, under its own rules; the scope of the data processed depends on the system and the device settings. The App receives only the recognised text, has no microphone permission and does not record sound itself. Neither the recording nor the recognised text is passed to the Publisher.

Android TV home screen (“Continue watching”)

On televisions and set-top boxes with Android TV and Google TV, the App adds films and episodes that have been started to the system “Continue watching” row. The following is passed: the title, the poster address, the duration and the place where you stopped watching. This data is saved by the device’s system and displayed by the television’s home screen supplied by the system manufacturer (for example Google). You can remove an item from the home screen or from the “Continue watching” list in the App.

Opening in the VLC app

If you choose “Open in VLC”, the App passes the address of the stream being played to the VLC app installed on your device. The address may contain the login details of your Content Source. From that moment the address is processed by VLC (VideoLAN), under its own rules (videolan.org/vlc/privacy.html); VLC may save it, for example in its history. Before you first use this function, the App displays a warning. If VLC is not installed, the App may open its page in Google Play.

“Cast to TV” (local network)

The function sends the content being played from a phone or tablet to a television with the VivaPilot App on the same local network. The phone sends to the television directly the stream address, the title, the playback position and – if they are set for the given Content Source – the User-Agent and Referer headers, and also the model name of the device you are sending from (shown in the consent window on the television). This is not a Google Cast service or any other external service, and the data does not reach the Publisher.

The function is intended solely for a trusted local network, for example a home network. The transmission is not encrypted and the stream address may contain login details, so other people on the same network can intercept it. The command requires acceptance on the television. Before you first use the function, the App displays a warning. Do not use it on public, hotel or company networks.

Backup

The “Export backup” function creates a file containing your Content Sources (including login details), lists and history and saves it in a location you specify. The file is encrypted with a password that you set when exporting. Without this password the backup cannot be read; the Publisher does not know it and cannot recover it. The backup contains login details: create it only when needed and protect it from access by other people.

Purchase of the Full Version

The purchase is handled by the Store (Google Play). The Store processes data related to the payment and your account under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The Publisher does not receive payment details. The App checks with the Store whether the Full Version has been purchased, and the Publisher receives from the Store the transaction data described in section 3.

The Store and the device system

The Store and the device system may collect data on the installation and operation of the App, under their own rules (for example crash reports, if sending of diagnostic data to Google is switched on in the Android settings). The Publisher receives only aggregate statistics from them (section 3).

6. Ads

1.This section applies to the release of the App with ads: the App’s page in the Store then carries the label “Contains ads”. On phones and tablets the Free Version then displays Google AdMob ads, supplied by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Ads are not displayed during the Trial Period, after the Full Version is purchased, or on televisions and set-top boxes with Android TV and Google TV.
2.The Google ad module is part of the release of the App with ads. In order to display ads, limit their repetition, measure their effectiveness and prevent abuse, Google processes, among other things, the device’s advertising identifier, the IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), information about the device and the App, and about interactions with ads.
3.The Publisher and Google are separate controllers. The Publisher decides on including the ad module in the App and on its settings (for example the ad categories) and is responsible for displaying the consent form. Google processes data for its own purposes, under the rules described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google’s data protection terms for controllers (business.safety.google/adscontrollerterms). Data may be transferred to the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
4.In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before ads are displayed for the first time, the App displays a Google consent form (User Messaging Platform), to the extent that Google makes it available in the given country. The form allows you to decide separately, among other things, on the storing of information on the device or access to it (for example to the advertising identifier) and on personalised ads. The basis for processing data for these purposes is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).
5.If the required consent is not given, the App uses only those ad display modes that are permitted by law and by the Google AdMob configuration. Non-personalised ads also require some data to be processed, for example the IP address to determine an approximate location, to limit the frequency of display and to prevent abuse.
6.You can change or withdraw your consents at any time in the App’s Settings (the “Ad privacy” option). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.
7.You can reset or delete the advertising identifier in the Android settings (for example Settings › Privacy › Ads; the names depend on the device).
8.The Publisher does not receive from Google data that would allow you to be identified, only aggregate statistics of impressions and ad revenue.

7. Analytics and other modules

1.The Publisher has not added analytics tools (for example Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) or tracking tools to the App. Data on the use of the App may be collected, under their own rules, by modules and services of other parties described in sections 5 and 6.

8. App permissions

The App uses the following permissions and functions of the Android system:

  • internet, network state and Wi-Fi: connecting to Content Sources, adjusting the buffer to the quality of the connection, resuming playback when the network returns;
  • discovery of devices on the local network (multicast): the “Cast to TV” function;
  • keeping the device awake: uninterrupted playback;
  • access to selected folders: playing video files from folders that you point to in the system selection window; the App does not browse other files;
  • notifications: controlling playback from the lock screen, headphones and the notification;
  • writing to the system TV guide: the “Continue watching” row on the television’s home screen;
  • picture-in-picture: minimising the player without interrupting viewing;
  • payments in Google Play: purchase of the Full Version;
  • advertising identifier (AD_ID): only in the release of the App with ads (section 6).

The App has no permissions for contacts, location, microphone, camera or SMS messages.

9. Transfers of data outside the European Economic Area

1.The Publisher does not transfer data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), with one exception: e-mail correspondence is handled by the Gmail service (Google), so it may also be processed in the USA. This takes place on the basis of a decision of the European Commission finding an adequate level of data protection (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.
2.Data that your device sends directly to other parties (the Content Source provider, the Store, Google in connection with ads and system services) is processed by those parties as separate controllers. They provide information on any transfers of data outside the EEA in their privacy policies. This is not a transfer of data by the Publisher.

10. Your rights

1.With regard to the data processed by the Publisher (section 3), you have the right to:
  • access the data and obtain a copy of it;
  • rectify the data;
  • erase the data;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability, for data that is processed on the basis of a contract by automated means;
  • object (paragraph 2).
2.Right to object. You may at any time object to the processing of your data on the basis of the Publisher’s legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), on grounds relating to your particular situation. The Publisher will then stop processing that data, unless the Publisher demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or grounds for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
3.You may withdraw at any time, in the App’s Settings (the “Ad privacy” option), the consents given in the ad consent form, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before their withdrawal (section 6).
4.These rights apply on the terms set out in the GDPR; for example, the right to erasure does not cover data that the Publisher must retain under tax law. To exercise your rights, write to the Publisher (section 1). The Publisher replies without undue delay, and no later than within one month of receiving the request. Where necessary, this period may be extended by a further two months; the Publisher will inform you of this within one month. If the Publisher has reasonable doubts as to your identity, it may ask for additional information.
5.You have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych; ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl) or with a supervisory authority in the Member State of the European Union in which you have your habitual residence or place of work.
6.You manage the data saved in the App on your device (section 4) yourself: you can view, change, delete and save it in a backup. You can view the record of acceptance of the Terms of Use and save it to a file, but you cannot change it.
7.For data processed by other parties (the Content Source provider, the Store, Google in connection with ads and system services), contact them directly.

11. Security

1.The Publisher protects the data that it receives: it secures access to its e-mail and to its accounts in the Store and in Google AdMob with two-step verification, does not keep data longer than stated in section 3, and does not make it available to unauthorised persons.
2.The App does not require an account and does not send to the Publisher the data saved on the device. It keeps that data in the App’s storage, access to which is subject to the protection mechanisms of the Android system, and encrypts the backup file with a password that you set. Passwords to Content Sources are not covered by the automatic Google backup, and the crash log contains no logins, passwords or tokens. Before you first use the “Cast to TV” and “Open in VLC” functions, the App warns about the risk.
3.The security of data on the device also depends on you: protect the device with a screen lock, do not share the backup file or its password, and install the App only from the Store.
4.If a personal data breach occurs in relation to the data processed by the Publisher, the Publisher acts in accordance with the GDPR, including, where necessary, notifying the President of the Personal Data Protection Office and the data subjects.

12. Minors

1.The App is intended for adults (18 years and older) and is not directed at children or young people. The Publisher does not knowingly collect data of minors. If children also use the device with the App, for example on a shared television, use the parental controls of the system or the Store. If the Publisher learns that it has received the data of a minor, for example in an e-mail message, it will delete it, unless it must retain it under the law.

13. Changes to the Policy

1.If the way data is processed changes, for example a function that connects to a new service appears, the Publisher will update the Policy and will give notice of this in the App and at https://dam-gal.github.io/vivapilot-legal/ before the change takes effect.
2.The current version of the Policy is available in the App (Settings › Legal information) and at https://dam-gal.github.io/vivapilot-legal/. The Policy is drawn up in Polish. Translations, where available, make it easier to understand; in the event of any discrepancy, the Polish version prevails.
