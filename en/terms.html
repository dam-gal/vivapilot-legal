§ 3. What the App is

1.The App is a media player. It plays video and audio streams from Content Sources entered by the User, and video files saved on the User’s device (the “Folder” function).
2.The App does not contain channels, films, series or any other content. It has no built-in Content Sources, channel lists or links to services that provide content. Search in the App covers only the catalogue downloaded from the Content Sources entered by the User. After installation, the App plays nothing until the User enters a Content Source or points to a video file.
3.The Publisher is not a content provider. The Publisher does not select, supply, store or moderate the content played in the App. The App connects to the Content Source directly from the User’s device. The Publisher does not operate a server that acts as an intermediary in this connection and does not receive information about which Content Sources the User enters.
4.The Publisher is not a party to the User’s contracts with providers of Content Sources, does not cooperate with such providers and does not recommend them. The App is not presented as a way of obtaining free access to paid content.
5.Some functions use the services of other parties. Voice search (the system speech recognition service) and opening content in the VLC app work only if the User uses them. On televisions and set-top boxes with Android TV and Google TV, the App adds films and episodes that have been started to the system “Continue watching” row on the home screen. The Free Version on phones and tablets may connect to the Google AdMob advertising network (§ 12). The data transmitted in connection with these functions is described in the Privacy Policy.

§ 5. Technical requirements and security

1.The following are needed to use the App:
  • a smartphone or tablet running Android 7.0 or later, or a television or set-top box with Android TV or Google TV running Android 7.0 or later, on which the Store allows the App to be installed; the App is not intended for devices without the Google Play store, for watches or for in-car systems;
  • an internet connection;
  • for viewing: a Content Source that the User may lawfully use, or video files saved on the device;
  • for purchasing the Full Version: a Google account.
2.The App supports Content Sources in the M3U/M3U8 and Xtream Codes formats and a programme guide in the XMLTV format. Whether a stream is played also depends on its format and on the codecs supported by the App’s player and the device. Playback of material encoded in the H.265/HEVC, AV1, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS or TrueHD standards depends on the device’s decoders and may not work on some devices. The App does not support material protected by DRM systems (for example Widevine, PlayReady); such material may not play, and the App may not indicate the reason. The App loads M3U/M3U8 playlists only from an internet address, not from a file saved on the device, and does not load compressed playlists (for example .gz) or external subtitle files. Details are provided in the “Guide and FAQ” at https://dam-gal.github.io/vivapilot-legal/.
3.The quality and continuity of playback also depend on the internet connection, the capabilities of the device and the operation of the Content Source provider’s server.
4.The following risks are associated with using the App:
  • the login details for Content Sources are saved in the App on the device and may be seen by other persons using that device;
  • the connection to the Content Source may not be encrypted if its provider’s server does not use encryption;
  • the “Cast to TV” function transmits, over the local network and without encryption, the stream address, which may contain login details; the function is intended solely for a trusted local network, and the command requires acceptance on the television;
  • opening content in the VLC app passes the stream address, which may contain login details, to that app;
  • the backup file contains the login details for Content Sources; it is encrypted with a password set by the User, which the Publisher does not know and cannot recover;
  • an App installed from outside the Store may have been altered by third parties, so it should be installed only from the Store.
