Guide and FAQ

Version 1.27

This document helps you use the App. It is not part of the Terms of Use or the Privacy Policy: the rules of use and purchase are set out in the Terms of Use, and the processing of data in the Privacy Policy. This text is a translation of the Polish version. In the event of any discrepancy, the Polish version prevails.

General

Where can I find the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy?

In the App: Settings › Legal information. On the website: https://dam-gal.github.io/vivapilot-legal/.

Where can I see the record of my acceptance of the Terms of Use, and how do I save it?

Settings › Legal information › My consents. The “Save consent confirmation” button saves it to a file.

Where can I find the confirmation of my purchase of the Full Version?

Settings › Legal information › Purchase. The Store also sends a confirmation to the e-mail address of your account.

How do I withdraw from the contract and get my money back?

You have 14 days from the purchase, even after the Full Version has been unlocked. Send a statement to dg.komunikacja@gmail.com or by post (the address is in the Terms of Use, § 1 para. 2). You can use the model form annexed to the Terms of Use. Give the date of purchase and the order number from the e-mail from the Store. The money will be returned within 14 days, by the same method that was used to pay.

Where are the licences of the open-source components?

Settings › Legal information › Open source licenses.

Which formats does the App support?

M3U and M3U8 lists, Xtream Codes login details and a programme guide in XMLTV format. Whether a given stream plays depends on its format and codec, on your device and on the Content Source. The App does not support material protected by DRM systems (for example Widevine, PlayReady). Details for your platform are below.

How do I make a backup?

Settings › BACKUP › Export backup. Choose a password for the backup and keep it in a safe place: without it the backup cannot be restored, and the Publisher does not know it. The backup contains the login details for your Content Sources, so do not pass it on to other people.

Android (Google Play)

What do the labels on the top bar and the padlocks mean?

The top bar shows: “Trial: N d.” (the number of days of the Trial Period that are left), “Free” (after the Trial Period without a purchase) or “Full” (after purchase). The label is for information only and blocks nothing. Tapping “Free” takes you to the purchase screen. Items available only in the Full Version have a padlock; tapping it opens the purchase window.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account that was used to buy the Full Version. The App checks the purchase automatically. If this does not work, write to dg.komunikacja@gmail.com.

How do I ask for a refund in Google Play?

You can also ask for a refund directly in Google Play, under Google’s rules: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. This does not limit the right of withdrawal from the contract.

How do I change my advertising consents?

Settings › Legal information › Ad privacy. This item is visible where the law requires consent (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland).

How do I switch off background refresh of the catalogue?

Settings › BACKGROUND REFRESH › switch off “Background catalogue refresh”.

What does the App on Android not support?

It loads M3U/M3U8 lists only from an internet address, not from a file saved on the device, and does not load compressed lists (for example .gz). It does not load external subtitle files; it shows the subtitles and audio tracks contained in the stream. Playback of material in the H.265/HEVC, AV1, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS and TrueHD standards depends on your device’s decoders. When a stream does not play, the App retries the connection and does not give the reason.

How does “Cast to TV” work and what does it require?

The television or set-top box with Android TV or Google TV must have the VivaPilot app installed and running. The phone or tablet and the television must be on the same local network (Wi-Fi), and the television asks for confirmation before playback. This is not Google Cast, so the function will not work with devices that do not have the VivaPilot app. The safety rules are described in the next question.

How do I use “Cast to TV” safely?

Only on a trusted home network. The stream address is sent without encryption and may contain login details. The television will ask for confirmation before playback.

What happens when I choose “Open in VLC”?

The App passes the stream address to the VLC app. The address may contain login details, and VLC may save it in its history.

How do I delete all of the App’s data?

System settings › Apps › VivaPilot › Storage › Clear data (the names may differ depending on the device) or uninstall the App.
