Tester guide

VivaPilot, closed test (Android)

Thank you for helping to test VivaPilot. It is a media player for phones, tablets and Android TV. The app contains no channels or content: for the test you will add a small demo list yourself by following the steps below. The app is intended for adults.

1. Join the test and install

1.On your Android phone, open the test link you received from me and tap “Become a tester” (signed in with the Google account you gave me for the tester list).
2.Tap the Google Play link and install the VivaPilot app.
3.Please do not uninstall the app or leave the test during the whole testing period (at least 14 days). Google requires this.

2. First launch

1.Tick the acceptance of the Terms of Use and tap Start.
2.The app has a 5-day trial with all features, then it works as the free version. The Full Version is a one-time purchase, but you do not need to buy anything for the test.

3. Add the demo list

1.Copy this address: https://dam-gal.github.io/vivapilot-legal/reviewer/test.m3u
2.In the app: Settings › Manage playlists › + › M3U / M3U8.
3.In the address field, long-press and choose “Paste” (or type the address). Tap Add.
4.Open the Live tab and choose “Test Channel 1”. A short test video should start.

The demo list contains live channels only, so the Movies and Series tabs will be empty. This is not a bug. You can also add your own M3U list or Xtream Codes login, but only from a source you have the right to use.

4. What to check

•Whether adding a list and playback work.
•Whether the menu, search, favourites and settings work without freezing or errors.
•Whether the texts are readable in your phone’s language (the app has 7 languages).
•If you have a TV with Android TV or Google TV: whether “Cast to TV” works (it needs the VivaPilot app on the TV and the same Wi-Fi network).

After the trial period the free version may show ads. Please do not click on ads.

5. How to report a problem

Write to dg.komunikacja@gmail.com. Please include: your phone model and Android version, what you did, what happened and, if you can, a screenshot. You can also use the tester feedback option in Google Play.

Thank you!
